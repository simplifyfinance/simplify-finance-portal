The email doesn’t know the scenario changed

Found it. It’s one missing link, and it affects the LO the same way.

What is actually happening

The email isn’t built when you look at it. It’s built once, when you press Generate email, and then the finished HTML is saved onto the deal like a photograph. Everything after that — the Preview tab, Copy to Outlook, Send to client — just shows you that photograph again.

You change the scenario
Investment purchase → First home buyer
→
The form updates
Right tabs, right fields, right splits. This half works.
→
The saved email doesn’t
Nobody tells the photograph to be retaken. It is still the investment email.
And nothing on screen says so. The Preview tab looks completely normal — same header, same “AI generated” label. There is no way to tell by looking that you are reading an email written for a scenario the deal is no longer on. If you press Send to client from there, that is what goes to the client.

Where it breaks, precisely: changing the scenario resets the loan splits and the notes, but it does not touch the saved email. Three lines of code do the swap, and the saved email isn’t one of them.

There is a second, quieter one

Even if you do press Generate again, some of the old scenario comes with it. The “Important things to note” wording is filled in from the scenario when you first pick it — for an investment that’s the line about assuming a 4% rental yield. When you switch to first home buyer, that wording is deliberately left alone, because the code can’t tell your typing from the default text and won’t overwrite a broker.

So a regenerated first-home-buyer email still tells a first home buyer about rental yield.

Same shape elsewhere — I checked

  • The LO email is identical. Same saved photograph, same missing link. Change the LO scenario and the LO preview stays on the old one.
  • The LO and the BC disagree with each other on the notes: the LO wipes your notes when you change scenario, the BC never does. Neither is obviously right, but they shouldn’t be different.
  • The Compliance email is safe — it’s built fresh every time, not saved.
  • One extra bug, unrelated but found on the way: the Custom (all fields) scenario crashes the page when you click it. It’s in the list of choices but has no loan-split setup behind it. Small fix.

What I’d do about it

Not silently regenerate — that would quietly rewrite an email you may have already read and approved. Instead, stamp the saved email with the scenario it was written for, and say so out loud when the two stop matching:

BC Form
Preview & share
⚠ This email was written for Investment purchase. The deal is now on First home buyer, so what’s below is out of date. Regenerate it before you send anything.
Email preview — ChapmanAI generated
Hi Sarah,

Thank you for the opportunity to assist with your investment purchase. Based on an assumed rental yield of 4% p.a. …

The old email stays visible — greyed, so you can still read what was there — but Send to client and Copy to Outlook are switched off until you regenerate.

And on the notes: when you change scenario, if the notes are still word for word the old scenario’s default wording, they get swapped for the new scenario’s. If you’ve typed anything of your own, they’re left exactly as they are and the banner mentions it. That way the rental-yield line goes when it should, and your writing never gets deleted.
The same fix covers the LO, since it’s the same shape. I’d do both together, plus the Custom crash.
