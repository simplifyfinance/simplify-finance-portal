Why it locks. Without it somebody re-ticks an add-back three weeks later and the compliance note still quotes the old figure — or the new one, and nobody can tell which. Locked, the note quotes a set that has a figure, a method, a time and a person against it. Change the documents and you unlock, say why, and that is on the record too.
