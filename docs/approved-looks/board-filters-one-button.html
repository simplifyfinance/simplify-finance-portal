What changed from the last one

One button instead of four. The row is what you already have plus Filters. Nothing sits open taking space, and the button carries a number when anything is on.

Each group folds. Broker, Credit officer and Lender each collapse, and the panel opens with everything folded except whatever you last used. Nine brokers and thirty lenders still fit.

What is on comes out as chips on the bar. So you can see and drop a filter without opening the panel at all — and the “showing 14 of 41” is still there, still unmissable.

An option that would empty the board is greyed rather than hidden, with its zero showing. Hiding it makes a lender look like it has no deals at all; greying it says “not with what you have already picked”.
