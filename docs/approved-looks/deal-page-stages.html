The stage bar is untouched. A white tick inside a filled green ball for everything behind you, the date under it, the green line joining them; a blue ring on the one you are on with its word underneath; grey and hollow for everything ahead. That design took you a long time to get right and I am not touching it.

The three notes boxes keep the sizes they have now. Needs action short, Sales notes tall enough for a real note, File update tall enough for a few entries. The only change inside them is the rule down the left of the writing and the handle at the foot.

Red is the triangle and one word, nowhere else. No red panels, no red borders, no red fields. Look at the BC stage for the one prompt that survives — nobody is down as doing the BC — drawn the quiet way.

Taller is gone. The two boxes that hold writing have a grab handle on the bottom edge instead, and the writing itself sits behind a rule with the date and the author above it, so it reads as somebody’s words rather than portal text.

The green line is on every stage from the moment the client agrees — what they agreed to, the date, and who recorded it. It is the one band worth keeping: nothing on it needs doing, it is a thing that went right, said once where the broker lands.

History is underlined in the deal box, beside All changes saved. You and Kylie both see it on every deal, every stage.

Send it back is back on the row, not hidden. Done, Send to client, Client agreed, Send it back, More. It is the one button that undoes somebody else's work, so it is the last thing I should have buried. More now holds only Order a valuation.

The questions stay where they are — under the stage, collapsed, exactly as they are today: asked date, days waiting, how many are going out, and Show. They belong to the email going out from that tab, and they carry the tick per question, the reason the portal found it, and Ask something else. None of that fits in the rail, and none of it should move. What the rail box carries is the count and the wait, with a link straight to the list.

The band is gone from every stage. You were right — it said the same sentence and pressed the same button as the box in the rail. The box carries it instead: when documents become due the count goes red and the Request button is the one filled thing in the box. One place, one press.

Every box has its one strong button back. Add on Needs action, Edit on Sales notes, Add note on File update, Request on what we need, Add on Links. Taller stays a quiet link, because it changes the view rather than doing anything.

Documents and questions are one box now — what we need from the client. Both are things asked of the same person. The purple band at the top is gone from every stage but one: it appears the day the client agrees to the BC, because that is the day eighteen documents genuinely become due. Step through the stages and watch the box fill and the band come and go.

Mark urgent moved to the top, beside Open BC, with the flag. You were right — it is not a thing that has gone wrong, it is a thing you are doing on purpose. Something wrong? keeps the three that really are faults: Credit cannot action, Report a problem, Close this deal.

Your concern about the static part as the deal moves. The deal box carries a row per person, and it gains one when the stage has one: Compliance pack — Ellesse Moran appears at compliance, Lender — Macquarie · ref 884 2201 appears once it is lodged. Same row, same Reassign, same place. Flip to Compliance and Settlement and watch it grow.

Everything that appears later has a home. Push to SalesTrekker and Flag an issue at compliance. Ready to settle, Book settlement and Outstandings at settlement, with the seven steps behind the Step picker. Google review sent, Compliance finalised and Commission paid when it settles, plus the commission figures. Nothing in the deal's life is left without a box.
