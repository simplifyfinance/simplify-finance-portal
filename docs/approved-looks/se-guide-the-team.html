2 — when what lands does not fit

A trust return turns up. The reader stops. It does not read three of the four and leave somebody to notice the fourth was ignored — that is how a trust distribution to a spouse goes missing from a file. It says what it found, why it cannot go on, and keeps what it did read so nothing is retyped from scratch.

The rule underneath all of this. The reader never half-does a deal. Either it read everything it needs and you get the comparison, or it stops and hands you to the route that can. A reader that quietly skips the document it did not understand is worse than no reader, because the figure still looks finished.
