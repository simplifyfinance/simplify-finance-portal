3

After the compliance pack has gone out

Up to that point you can switch freely. Once a figure has been quoted to someone outside this office, the other buttons go quiet and changing it asks you why — because a credit note already in SalesTrekker says $170,000 and somebody will have to explain the difference.

The lending options emailWhatever the client is told borrows from the same figure.

Why switching stays easy until it does not. Locking it at the first press would mean unlocking it four times on a normal deal, and anything you have to unlock four times is something people learn to leave alone. Nothing has left the office until compliance goes out, so until then changing your mind costs one press. After that it costs a sentence, which is right, because somebody outside has the old number.
