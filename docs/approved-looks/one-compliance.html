The one real changeKnowing what is missing without opening five tabs

Today the five sections are a full width bar of equal buttons, and the only way to find an empty box is to open each one — or to press Compliance complete and read the list of errors it refuses you with. That count already exists for the error list. It is simply not shown anywhere until you have pressed the button and been told no.

Option A puts the number on the tab. Red while something is missing, a green tick when the section is finished. Nothing new is worked out — it is the same check that already blocks completion.

What I did not doThree things left alone on purpose

The AI stamps stay as they are. AI generated, checked by hand, out of date — those already exist and already mean something specific on a regulated document. They are drawn here, not changed.

Living expenses keeps its three-way answer. In HEM, not in HEM, to answer. Turning it into a tick box would lose the difference between "not in HEM" and "nobody has said".

Nothing moves into the rail. Compliance has no strips above the tabs to lift out — that was the Fact Find and BC problem, and it is already done.
