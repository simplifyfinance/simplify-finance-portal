And the figures go in the fields that already exist. The portal already holds seven boxes per year — salary, net profit, depreciation, interest, superannuation, one-off and other — for two financial years. The reader fills those in instead of a person typing them. That is the whole change: same fields, same maths, same compliance wording, read off the document rather than keyed.

One thing I checked and want to flag. Year 1 in the data is the EARLIER year and Year 2 is the later one — picking year 1 sets year 2 to the year after it. “One year in isolation” reads year 1, which is right only because that method hides year 2 and you put the single year in slot 1. It works. But it is the kind of thing that breaks the day somebody makes year 2 visible on that method, so when we build this I would put a test on it rather than leave it to be rediscovered.
