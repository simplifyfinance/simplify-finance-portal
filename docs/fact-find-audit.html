What the real deal exposed

This is the part that matters. The map below is theory; these are faults found by taking the actual Alexis Janes record and reading the email that went out against what the Fact Find holds.

1. Eight mortgages reported to the client as “Balance $0”. The email said Linked loan: Pepper Money — Balance $0 against every one of her eight properties. The Fact Find records those loans by limit — $506,514, $360,000, $452,000 and so on — with the balance box left empty, and the email printed the empty balance as zero. Fixed. It now prints the balance if there is one, the limit if there is not, and nothing at all if neither. A figure that is not recorded is not zero.
2. A fourth hand-written money formatter, with the same fault as the other three. fmtMoney() in the BC used Number(v), which turns "506,514" into nothing and returns the string "0". Two more identical copies were sitting in the deal summary page and the reports page. Fixed — all three gone, and a new ship gate fails the build if anybody writes another one.
3. Two placeholders are going out in client emails. Her email contains the literal text Against [Property Address] and Estimated repayments [calculated]. The address one happens because a refinance has no “suburb” recorded on the BC — the eight real addresses are sitting in the Fact Find, unused. The repayment one is a figure nothing ever works out. Not fixed. Both change what a client sees, so they need Fabio's eyes and a mockup first.
4. “Existing loan balance” is blank in the email. The BC's existingLoanBal is empty on this deal, so the row prints with nothing after it. It only ever fills itself from a loan somebody has ticked as “to be refinanced”, and none of Alexis's eight loans is ticked. Not fixed — same reason.
And one that is now right. The income line in that same email reads “Alexis — PAYG income $146,380 p.a.”. Yesterday it said $0, on every deal in the portal.

Boxes that go nowhere

  • Middle name — collected on the form, written by the AI extractor, read by nothing. Lenders ask for full legal names, so this probably belongs in the broker notes rather than being removed.
  • “Is the company profitable?” (a self-employed question) — answered by staff, used by nothing at all. Either it should affect the assessable income or it should come off the form.
  • Financial year 1 and 2 — the labels of the years a self-employed income is assessed on. A lender wants to know which two years the figure came from; nothing prints them.

The map

A tick means that box is read by that part of the portal. Blank means it is not. One honest limit: the fifteen self-employed year figures are read through a name the code builds up as it goes, so a literal search cannot see them — they are marked as read by the income maths, verified by hand. Everything else was found by looking for a real read of the box.

What I would do next

  • The two placeholders. A client email should never contain square brackets. Mockup first.
  • Property addresses on a refinance. Eight real addresses in the Fact Find and the email says “[Property Address]”. The security address for a refinance should come from the properties.
  • Loan balance vs limit. Staff record one or the other. Everything downstream should say which it has, and nothing should ever assume the missing one is zero. Fixed in the client email; the same pattern needs checking in the broker notes, the compliance notes and the handover.
  • The homeless boxes. Three of them. Each either gets a home or comes off the form.
  • Then the same audit for the BC, the Lending Options and the Compliance tabs — this one covers the Fact Find only.
