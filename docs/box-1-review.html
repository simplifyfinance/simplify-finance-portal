The approved example — figures swapped

Why the numbers look odd

Hand a model a worked example and it borrows from it — a suburb, a lender, a figure. So the real ones are replaced with values no deal will ever have: $1,111,000 · $777,000 · 70% · age 44 · $111,000 · Exampleton · Example Bank. If any of those ever surfaces in a real answer, it is obvious at a glance and we know the fence failed.

The applicant is seeking finance to purchase an investment property in Exampleton, NSW valued at approximately $1,111,000. She is requesting a loan amount of $777,000 over a 30-year term, representing a conservative LVR of approximately 70%.

The applicant has requested a split loan structure comprising both Interest Only and Principal & Interest repayments. The Interest Only split will assist with cash flow flexibility, while the Principal & Interest split will facilitate debt reduction and equity accumulation from settlement.

The applicant prefers Example Bank and requires a variable rate loan with offset, redraw and online banking functionality. While Example Bank is the preferred lender, the applicant is willing to consider alternative lenders that meet her requirements and provide a suitable overall outcome.

Funds for the purchase will be sourced from existing savings and the sale of shares. The applicant intends to retain a cash buffer after settlement to provide ongoing financial flexibility and cover unforeseen expenses.

The applicant is a 44-year-old full-time Office Manager earning approximately $111,000 per annum. She has two dependent children under a shared custody arrangement, stable employment, a strong asset position and no adverse credit history.

The applicant is not a first home buyer.

The two to talk about first

Custody arrangement. The example says “two dependent children under a shared custody arrangement”. The portal records the number of dependants and nothing else — no custody, no ages. So that clause can only ever come from the broker writing it in the goals boxes. Either we accept it will usually be absent, or the fact find needs to ask.

Savings retained after settlement. “Intends to retain a cash buffer” is a real compliance point, and we can only state it when assets exceed the funds to complete. Worth deciding whether the model says nothing when it cannot see a surplus, or says the buffer is not recorded.
